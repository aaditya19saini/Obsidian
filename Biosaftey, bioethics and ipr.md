# UBT614: Biosafety, Bioethics & IPR - Comprehensive Study Checklist

Based on the course materials and the exam papers from **March and May 2025**, here is the high-intensity study checklist for your exam tomorrow.

---

## 🛑 Phase 1: The "Deep Dive" (Must Know Verbatim)

These represent the core "Saxena-style" questions. You must reproduce these with technical precision and hierarchical structure.

-  **[ ] Indian Regulatory Hierarchy (Rules 1989):** Memorize the full names and specific functions of **RDAC** (Advisory), **IBSC** (Institutional), **RCGM** (Reviewing), and **GEAC** (Appraisal/Approval) under the **Environment Protection Act (EPA)**.
    
- **[ ] Biosafety Levels (BSL 1-4):** Master the **Basis of Classification**: Pathogenicity, Mode of Transmission, Host Range, and Availability of Treatment/Prophylaxis.
    
- **[ ] IPR & Patenting Criteria:** Memorize the "Three Pillars" of patentability in India: **Novelty**, **Inventive Step (Non-obviousness)**, and **Industrial Application**.
    
- **[ ] Patent Filing Flowchart:** Be able to draw the schematic from **Provisional Specification** $\rightarrow$ **Complete Specification** $\rightarrow$ **Publication** $\rightarrow$ **Examination** $\rightarrow$ **Grant**.
    
- **[ ] The Richard Din Case:** Detail the agent (_N. meningitidis_), the failure (open-bench work instead of BSC), and the lack of institutional oversight.
    
- **[ ] The Amerithrax (2001 Anthrax) Case:** Focus on the mode of spread (postal system), clinical symptoms of **Inhalation Anthrax**, and the forensic diagnosis.

## ⚠️ Phase 2: "Thorough Review" (High Probability)

These require a detailed understanding and the ability to list specific criteria.

- **[ ] Cartagena Biosafety Protocol:** Focus on the **Precautionary Principle**, **Advance Informed Agreement (AIA)**, and the **Biosafety Clearing-House (BCH)**.
    
- **[ ] Surrogacy (Regulation) Bill:** Contrast the **"Certificate of Eligibility"** (for the surrogate) and the **"Certificate of Essentiality"** (for the intending couple).
    
- **[ ] Laboratory Equipment Mechanics:** Explain the "relevance" of **Biosafety Cabinets (BSCs)**, **Autoclaves**, and **Centrifuge Safety Cups** in preventing **Laboratory-Acquired Infections (LAIs)**.
    
- **[ ] Human Genome Project vs. Genome India Project:** Contrast the global HGP (1990-2003) with India's specific goal of sequencing **10,000 whole genomes** to map local genetic diversity.
    
- **[ ] Ethical Failures in Gene Therapy:** Study the **Jesse Gelsinger case** (safety violations/adenoviral vector) and the **Rhys Evans case** (leukemia/retroviral vector).

## 🔍 Phase 3: "Skim & Understand" (Short Notes)

Skim these to ensure you can write 100–200 words for short-note questions.

- **[ ] GMO Risks:** Understand **Gene Flow** (Vertical vs. Horizontal), **Allergenicity**, and the impact on non-target organisms.
    
- **[ ] GURT (Terminator Technology):** Know the difference between **V-GURT** (sterile seeds) and **T-GURT** (trait-specific activation).
    
- **[ ] WHO Definition of Biosafety:** "The containment principles, technologies, and practices that are implemented to prevent unintentional exposure to pathogens and toxins, or their accidental release".
    
- **[ ] Types of IP:** Briefly distinguish between **Trademarks**, **Copyrights**, **Trade Secrets**, and **Geographical Indicators (GI)**.
    
- **[ ] Synthetic Biology Ethics:** Review the "minimal synthetic cell" experiments and the dual-use research concern.
