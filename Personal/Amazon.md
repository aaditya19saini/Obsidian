# **The interview will be almost entirely behavioral (LP-based)**

Amazon maps every question to their 16 Leadership Principles. The most relevant ones for this role are:

1. **Customer Obsession** – decisions driven by customer impact
2. **Ownership** – going beyond your scope, long-term thinking
3. **Bias for Action** – acting fast with incomplete info
4. **Dive Deep** – using data, not assumptions
5. **Deliver Results** – finishing what you started despite obstacles
6. **Invent and Simplify** – improving a process
7. **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit** – pushing back respectfully

---

 # **Your prep action (do this tonight)**

Pick 5-6 strong stories from your life (college projects, clubs, part-time work, anything). Each story should map to at least one LP. Structure every story in STAR format — be specific, use "I" not "we", and always end with a measurable result.

Suggested story themes to cover:

- A time you identified and fixed a problem nobody else noticed (Dive Deep + Ownership)
- A time you led or coordinated a team under pressure (Deliver Results)
- A time you disagreed with someone and handled it (Have Backbone)
- A time you did something faster than expected (Bias for Action)
- A time you improved an existing process (Invent and Simplify)
