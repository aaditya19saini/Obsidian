**Generalization** means how well a model that has been trained on one dataset can make accurate predictions on **new, unseen data**.

Break Down:
1. Training: Model learns pattern from the training data 
2. generalization: Model applies those learned patterns to fresh data 
3. The model either overfits or underfits depending upon the generalization
